Coffeeಕಾಫಿ

Berry development

Keep shade-tree drains open so water doesn't stand around the roots, and gather fallen berries to break the berry borer's cycle.

Black pepperಕಾಳುಮೆಣಸು

Spike setting

Yellowing vines after this much rain often mean quick wilt. Check the base of the vine, and don't apply fertiliser until the soil drains.

Arecanutಅಡಿಕೆ

Fruit-rot risk

Koleroga spreads fast in continuous rain. Members with nuts dropping should spray in the next dry window. Cover the bunches if you can.
